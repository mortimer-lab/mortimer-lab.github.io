My name is Jamie Markowski and I am a third year undergraduate student from Frenchtown, New Jersey. I am majoring in Microbiology, minoring in Public Health and Entomology, and working towards an Infectious Diseases certificate. I have been fascinated by microorganisms for as long as I can remember, and I am particularly interested in global infectious diseases and genetics, which the Mortimer Lab researches. Through my research, I am strengthening my computational skills and knowledge in the field of genomics. After my undergraduate years, I plan on furthering my education at graduate school with the eventual goal of working in the field of Public Health. Outside of the lab, I enjoy hiking, cooking, spending time with friends and family, and traveling.
